SEO & Rankings
How long does SEO take to show results?+
Honestly: local SEO typically shows meaningful results in 6–12 weeks. Some businesses see Google Maps improvements in 3–4 weeks. Google rankings for website content usually take 2–4 months for competitive keywords. Anyone who promises page-one rankings in 2 weeks is not being honest with you. We track and report progress monthly so you can see the trajectory clearly.
What's the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?+
Regular SEO targets people searching nationally — e.g. "best running shoes." Local SEO targets people searching near your business — e.g. "café near Kothrud" or "plumber in Viman Nagar." This is what most local businesses need, and it's what we specialise in. When someone nearby Googles what you offer, local SEO makes sure you appear at the top.
What is Google Business and why does it matter?+
Google Business Profile is the listing that shows on Google Maps and in the panel on the right side of Google when someone searches for your business or businesses like yours. It shows your address, hours, phone, photos, and reviews. For local businesses, this is often the single most important thing to get right. We set it up, optimise it, and keep it active on maintenance plans.
