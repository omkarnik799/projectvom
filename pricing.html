04 — Common Questions

Pricing questions,
answered.

Is the ₹6,000 really a one-time cost?
+
Yes — absolutely. ₹6,000 covers the complete setup of your website, domain, hosting configuration, Google Business setup, and local SEO foundation. You pay it once. There are no hidden setup fees, no onboarding charges, and nothing else due at launch.
What does the free first month include?
+
The free first month gives you whichever plan you choose — at zero cost. So if you choose Growth, you get a full month of active SEO campaigns, content updates, keyword tracking, and all Growth features before paying anything for maintenance.
Can I cancel my monthly plan?
+
Yes, anytime. No notice period, no cancellation fee. Your website, domain, and content belong to you — so you keep everything even if you cancel.
Can I switch plans later?
+
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect from your next billing cycle. Most clients start on Local or Growth and upgrade when they see results and want to scale faster.
Does ₹6,000 include domain and hosting costs?
+
The setup fee covers domain registration and hosting configuration. Ongoing domain renewal (typically ₹800–1,500/year) and hosting costs are passed through at cost with no markup — or included in your plan depending on which one you choose.
Are there any contracts to sign?
+
No long-term contracts. The setup is a one-time payment. Monthly plans are month-to-month. We earn your business every month — that's how we think it should work.
