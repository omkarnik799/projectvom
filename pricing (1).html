Quick Answers

Common questions.

Is there a contract or lock-in? +
No. Monthly plans are cancel-anytime. We earn your continued business by delivering results — not by trapping you in agreements.
Can I upgrade my plan later? +
Yes, anytime. Just message us on WhatsApp and we'll switch you over from the next billing month. No penalties for switching or cancelling.
What if I only need the website? +
That's fine. Pay ₹6,000, we build and hand over everything. The free month still applies. No obligation to continue any maintenance plan afterward.
Is the free month genuinely free? +
Yes. No credit card required, no automatic billing, no conditions. Every new client gets one full month of their chosen plan at ₹0. After that you decide.
Do you serve businesses outside Pune? +
We can work with businesses anywhere in India, though our local SEO expertise is strongest for Pune and surrounding areas. We'll be upfront about what we can deliver for your specific location.
What payment methods do you accept? +
UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and all major Indian payment methods. Proper invoices for every payment. GST invoicing available on request.
