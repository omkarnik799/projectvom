01 — Our Story

Two people.
One mission.

VOM is Viresh and Om — two Pune-based founders on a mission to put every great local business where it belongs: online, visible, and growing.

02 — Why We Started

Pune has incredible
businesses. Most are invisible online.

We exist to change that — one business at a time, permanently and properly.

We kept seeing it: brilliant restaurants, skilled professionals, dedicated shop owners — all building genuinely great businesses, but losing customers to weaker competitors who simply showed up on Google first.

So we built VOM. A lean, focused agency that uses automation and smart systems to deliver the results of a big agency — at a price that makes sense for Pune's businesses. No fluff, no jargon, no surprise invoices.

We're not a vendor. We're your digital partner. We treat your online presence the same way you treat your business — with care, consistency, and a genuine commitment to results.
