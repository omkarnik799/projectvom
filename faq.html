Local SEO
How long does SEO take to show results?
+
Local SEO results typically start appearing within 4–8 weeks for Google Maps and local pack rankings, with more significant keyword movement in 2–4 months. The timeline depends on your competition in Pune. We report progress every month.
What does "local SEO" actually mean?
+
Local SEO means getting your business to appear when people in Pune search for what you offer — both in Google's regular results and in Google Maps. It involves optimising your Google Business Profile, building local citations, targeting the right keywords, and making sure your website signals to Google that you serve Pune customers.
Can you guarantee Google rankings?
+
No — and anyone who guarantees specific rankings is not being honest with you. Google's algorithm isn't something any agency controls. What we can guarantee is that we follow proven best practices and report honestly on what's working.
Do I need a Google Business Profile?
+
Yes — if you serve local customers in Pune, your Google Business Profile is one of the most valuable digital assets you own. It's what drives your business to appear in Maps results and the local pack at the top of search results. We set it up and manage it as part of every plan.
What's the difference between Local SEO and Google Ads?
+
Local SEO is organic — you earn rankings over time, and they persist without ongoing ad spend. Google Ads is paid — you appear instantly at the top but stop appearing the moment you stop paying. We recommend SEO as the foundation, with Ads as an accelerant for specific campaigns.
The Website
Are the websites built on WordPress or a template?
+
Neither. We build custom-coded websites — clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — without page builder plugins, bloated themes, or template dependencies. This makes your site faster, more secure, and fully in your control.
Will my website work on mobile?
+
100%. Every site we build is mobile-first — meaning we design for phones first, then scale up to desktop. This is critical for Google rankings, which now use mobile page performance as a primary ranking signal.
How fast will my website load?
+
We target a Google PageSpeed Insights score above 90 for all our sites. Fast loading is built into how we code — no unnecessary scripts, optimised images, proper caching. Fast sites rank better and convert better.
Can I update my website myself?
+
If you need a content management system so you can update the site yourself, we can build one in. For most small businesses, it's more efficient to have us handle updates (included in all monthly plans) — so you don't have to learn a CMS and risk breaking your SEO.
Can you build an online store?
+
Yes — e-commerce is part of our Scale plan. We build fast online stores with product pages, inventory management, and payment gateway integration (Razorpay, Paytm, Stripe).
